Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who we are

Jetschool operates jetschoolusa.com. We are an aircraft brokerage. We help clients compare aircraft, prepare for a purchase, and arrange charter travel.

What you send us

When you ask to buy an aircraft, arrange a charter, request an advisory session, or send a message, we receive the details you choose to provide. That may include your name, email, phone number, the aircraft you are considering, the trip you describe, and your message.

You can use the research tools without sending us those details.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply, to prepare a purchase or charter conversation, and to keep a record of the request. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep a request while we are working with you and for as long as we need it for our records. You may ask us to delete it by writing to the address below. We will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@jetschoolusa.com
