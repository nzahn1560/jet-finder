Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who we are

JetSchool ("we", "us") operates jetschoolusa.com, an aviation marketplace and aircraft performance platform.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, company, phone number, and a hashed password when you create an account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and name from Google.
  • Listing content: aircraft details, photos, videos, and contact information you choose to publish in a listing.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used for security and debugging.
  • Contact messages: the name, email, phone number, and message you send through the contact form, so we can read and respond to it.

How we use it

  • To operate your account, listings, and purchases.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. password resets, listing status updates).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google — optional sign-in (OAuth).
  • Google — a tag for container GTM-TVGS9R2S loads on every page. It can record page views and the named events this site sends. The words typed into a contact message are not sent to Google.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Cloudflare — DNS, CDN, and media storage (R2).
  • Railway — application and database hosting.

Your choices

Signed-in account holders can open Settings to turn off optional emails, change a password, download a copy of their account information, or delete the account. Password resets, payment problems, and decisions about your own account still arrive by email. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. Delete the account from Settings, or write to the email below.

Cookies

We use a session cookie (jet_session) to keep you logged in. It is HttpOnly and not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. The Google tag may set its own cookies to count visits. Those cookies are separate from the login cookie.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@jetschoolusa.com
