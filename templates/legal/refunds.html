Refunds

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page states the same rule as the Terms of Service.

Research and inquiries

Comparing aircraft, planning a charter, and sending an inquiry on this website are free. Jetschool does not take payment on this website for an aircraft purchase or a charter. There is nothing to refund for using those tools.

A later professional fee

If you and Jetschool later agree in writing to a professional fee, that agreement states the amount and whether any part of it can be returned. A question, by itself, is not a promise that a refund will be issued.

How to ask

Write to support@jetschoolusa.com if you believe a fee was charged in error. We will review the request. A review is not a promise that money will be returned.
