Refunds

Last updated: October 2026

Payments on JetSchool are processed by Stripe. This page states the same refund rule as the Terms of Service.

Listing fees

  • Listing fees are non-refundable once a listing has been approved and published.
  • Submitting a listing, or paying the listing fee, does not by itself publish the listing. It stays in review until it is approved.
  • If a listing has not been approved and published, you may email support@jetschoolusa.com and ask for a review. A review is not a promise that a refund will be issued.

Broker access

Broker and brokerage fees are billed for the period you selected and continue until the subscription is cancelled. Cancelling stops the next renewal. A charge that has already been collected is not automatically refunded.

What this page does not do

JetSchool does not treat a question, a failed card, or an unpublished listing as a refund that is already owed. Stripe is the record of whether money was captured or returned. When Stripe reports that a captured charge was refunded, that payment record is updated. The listing stays in the state an administrator set.
