About Jetschool

Jetschool is an aircraft brokerage. We help clients compare aircraft, prepare for a purchase, and arrange charter travel. The research tools are free and do not require an account.

  • Aircraft comparisons for a purchase
  • Purchase advisory
  • Charter planning through authorized operators
  • A free advisory session when you want to talk it through

A price on this site is an average market figure for a model. It is not an offer for a specific airplane. Jetschool arranges charter travel and is not the operator of the flight.
