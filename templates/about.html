About Jetschool

Jetschool helps buyers and charter customers choose an aircraft with performance data, operating costs, and mission requirements. The research tools are free and do not require an account.

  • Independent aircraft comparisons
  • Data-driven purchasing decisions
  • Free aircraft research
  • Professional aircraft acquisition assistance
  • Charter planning and booking assistance through authorized operators

Jetschool is not an aircraft marketplace, and it is not the operator of charter flights. A model page is not a promise that a particular airplane is for sale or available on a date.
